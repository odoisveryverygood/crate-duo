# X post: CRATE

## Main post (attach crate_twitter.mp4)

I typed "french jazz piano sample" into my iPhone Duo and it made the beat in 0.3 seconds.

CRATE is an AI sampler for the Duo:
- digs your own sample packs instantly
- GPT arranges while it plays
- the hinge is the FX knob. Fold to build, snap open to drop

Won 1st place at the YC × @bitrig iPhone Duo hackathon 🏆

## Reply 1 (how it works)

How it's that fast: the sample library is pre-analyzed on device, so "digging" is a local search. A tiny typed model (Jev) reads your prompt in ~150 ms, the beat starts playing, and GPT writes the full arrangement in the background and swaps it in on the next bar.

## Reply 2 (the Duo part)

Every Duo feature does something musical:
- two screens = lid display + MPC-style pads
- hinge angle = punch-in filter, snap = DROP
- back screen = a light show for the crowd while you play
