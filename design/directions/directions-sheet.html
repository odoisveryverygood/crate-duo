Lid rules, all seven

Answering "too much text noise" and "hard to see".
  1. One hero row. BPM 100 · Swing 57 · Bar 4.1. The old header is gone.
  2. One title line. Style + sample; chords keep their case (A#m7, not A#M7).
  3. The grid. 6 lanes × 16 steps with a playhead you can't miss: a line or an inverted column.
  4. One result line + one timing strip. Plain language, no truncation, no ellipses.
  5. Prompt + chips at the foot. No log panel, no repeated header.
Deck state in every direction: SEQ mode, bank A, playing, pad 05 BRT HAT pressed, the ✦ DIG key.
